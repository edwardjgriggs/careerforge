# Trademark Policy

The CareerForge **source code** is licensed under Apache 2.0, which grants broad rights to use,
modify, and redistribute it. The Apache 2.0 license explicitly does **not** grant trademark
rights (§6), and this document explains what that means in practice.

The goal is narrow: people should be free to build on CareerForge, and users should be able to
tell what is official. Those two things are not in tension, and this policy protects the second
without limiting the first.

## What the name covers

"CareerForge", the CareerForge logo, and any confusingly similar variation.

## You do not need permission to

- Use, modify, fork, redistribute, or sell software built on CareerForge, under Apache 2.0.
- State truthfully that your project is **built on**, **compatible with**, **for**, or **a fork
  of** CareerForge.
- Name a plugin `careerforge-collector-jira`, `jira-for-careerforge`, or similar, provided it is
  clear you are not the CareerForge project.
- Use the name in articles, talks, tutorials, reviews, comparisons, and academic work.
- Use the name in community spaces — meetups, forums, discussion groups — that are clearly
  unofficial.

Nominative use is fine. Describing what your software does is not something we intend to police.

## Please do not

- Present a modified version, fork, or distribution as the **official** CareerForge.
- Use "CareerForge" as the primary name of your product, service, or company.
- Register domains, social accounts, or package names implying official status
  (`careerforge.com`, `@careerforge`, `getcareerforge`, `careerforge-official`).
- Use the logo as your own project's logo, or a confusingly similar mark.
- Imply endorsement, affiliation, certification, or partnership that does not exist.
- Use the name for a hosted service in a way suggesting it is operated by this project.

## Forks

Forking is welcome and expected — it is a right the license grants deliberately (see ADR-0003 and
`Vision.md` §9).

**Please rename your fork.** Keep an accurate attribution such as _"based on CareerForge"_ or
_"a fork of CareerForge"_, and remove the CareerForge logo from your branding. That is all we
ask.

## Plugins and integrations

Third-party plugins may use "CareerForge" descriptively in their names. The plugin index labels
each entry **Core**, **Official**, **Verified**, **Community**, or **Archived**. Only entries
maintained under the CareerForge organization may describe themselves as _official_.

Those labels describe review and maintenance status, never capability. A Community plugin is not
a lesser plugin — it is an unreviewed one.

## Why this exists

CareerForge asks users to trust it with a decade of their professional history and with access to
work product under NDA. That trust depends on being able to tell whether the thing you installed
is the thing that was audited.

Trademark is the right tool for that. Restricting the software license would be the wrong one,
which is why we did not.
